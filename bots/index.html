What these numbers mean
Forecast gap
Forecast chance of rain minus market price, in points. Forecast 74%, market 47c = a 27-point gap.
Settlement
The official end of a contract. Final daily airport rain of 0.01 inch or more pays YES $1 per contract; otherwise NO pays $1.
Fees
Exchange charges per order, included in amount spent and subtracted from profit.
Amount spent
Buy cost including buy fees.
Profit
Sell proceeds or settlement payout, minus sell fees and amount spent. Actual profit counts finished trades.
ROI
Profit divided by amount spent on those same finished trades.
If held (hypothetical)
The alternative result with no early sale, held to settlement. Never added to actual profit.
Total spent so far
Every buy, open or closed. This is not current risk or available cash.
Open right now
Amount spent on positions not yet sold or settled; money at risk. Resting buy reservations are not included.
